## 1. Source-derived conformal template

The mathematical template is taken from the author's note `Riemann_1overz_Baginski_3pager.pdf`.

The note centers the Riemann variable at one half,

\[
w = 2s-1,
\]

so that

\[
s \leftrightarrow 1-s
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
w \leftrightarrow -w.
\]

With

\[
z(s)=\frac{s-1}{s},
\]

\[
z(1-s)=\frac{1}{z(s)}.
\]

The same chart gives

\[
\Re(s)=\frac12
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
|z(s)|=1.
\]

On the unit circle,

\[
z^{-1}=\bar z.
\]

These identities are implemented directly by `RiemannConformalTemplateReceipt` and are tested numerically over critical-line and off-line samples.
